📚 What is Caesar Cipher?

Caesar Cipher is one of the oldest and simplest encryption techniques in cryptography. It is a substitution cipher where each letter in the plaintext is shifted by a fixed number of positions in the alphabet.

For example, with a shift of 3, A becomes D, B becomes E and C becomes F.

Encryption Formula:
E(x) = (x + k) mod 26

Decryption Formula:
D(x) = (x - k) mod 26
